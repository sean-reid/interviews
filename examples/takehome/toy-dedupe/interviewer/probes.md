# Live review probes

The submission is not the interview. This is.

- Walk me through what you keep in memory at peak. What is the ceiling?
- You evict after the window. What is the failure that causes, and how often?
- Where in your code would a duplicate get through? Show me the line.
- You used an AI assistant. What did you ask it, and what did you have to
  correct?
- If the window assumption were wrong, what would you change first?
